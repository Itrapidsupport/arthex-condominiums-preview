Order status certificate

Status certificates, ordered online.

Arthex provides secure, efficient processing of condominium status certificates for buyers, sellers, and legal representatives, through CONDUIT.

  1. 1

    Search the corporation

    Visit statuscertificate.com, where you can search 24/7 for the property by corporation number or address.

  2. 2

    Set up your CONDUIT login

    If this is your first time using CONDUIT, set up your user ID and password. Record them for future use; you will need them to access your certificate once completed.

  3. 3

    Order and pay online

    Fill in the required forms online and pay $128.25 by credit card: $100.00 for the status certificate and $28.25 charged by the web service provider as a convenience fee.

  4. 4

    Download the PDF

    Once your order is processed you will be notified by email that the certificate may be downloaded. Save the PDF and email it to the agent, lawyer and owner.

Total online price

$128.25

$100.00 certificate + $28.25 provider convenience fee

Status certificates are often completed and delivered electronically in two business days; more complicated certificates may take longer. You should always receive your status certificate within 10 days as per the Condominium Act, 1998.
