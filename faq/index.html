Boards and owners

Are you insured and certified?+

Yes, we have $5,000,000 liability insurance, $2,000,000 errors and omissions insurance, and all managers are bonded. We are ACMO 2000 certified and operate in compliance with Ontario regulations and best practices.

How do I access the community website?+

Email info@arthex.ca for login credentials and portal access.

How do I get an account statement?+

Email info@arthex.ca with your full property address. A copy will be sent to you.

How do I update my Pre-Authorized Payment (PAP) banking information?+

Email info@arthex.ca with your full property address to update banking details. The team will assist with the update.

Maintenance and emergencies

Do you provide emergency support?+

Yes, we have a 24-hour emergency line and regular office hours Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.

Who do I contact in case of an emergency?+

For fire, flood, or life-threatening situations, call 911. For building-related emergencies after hours (e.g. no heat, major leak), call our emergency line 905-340-0622 and press 1.

How do I submit a maintenance request?+

You can submit a maintenance request by email to info@arthex.ca, or by phone at 905-340-0622. For emergencies such as flooding, no heat in winter, or safety hazards, please call our emergency line immediately.

What qualifies as an emergency repair?+

Emergency repairs are issues that pose an immediate risk to health, safety, or property. Examples include no heat in winter, major leaks, sewage backups, electrical hazards, or being locked out. Non-urgent issues (e.g. minor leaks, appliance issues) will be scheduled during business hours.

Status certificates and vendors

How do I purchase a status certificate?+

Visit order.statuscertificate.com and search using the corporation number.

Can I get an update on the status of a payment or invoice?+

Ensure invoices are submitted through SparcPay. For instructions, email info@arthex.ca. If payment is submitted, forward a copy of the invoice to lc@arthex.ca for a status update.
