About Arthex

A third-generation firm, serving Ontario since 1972.

For more than five decades, Arthex Property Management (1983) Inc. has delivered trusted, professional, and responsive property management services to condominium corporations, rental property owners, and commercial clients across Ontario.

Company profile

Three chapters, one name.

  1. 1972

    The firm is founded

    Arthex was founded by Arthur Heximer, hence the name, becoming one of the first property management companies specializing in managing corporations in Ontario.

  2. 1983

    Arthex Property Management (1983)

    Wayne Campbell R.C.M. acquired the company and incorporated the name style Arthex Property Management (1983).

  3. 2008

    A new generation

    After twenty-five years, Mr. Campbell sold the company to property management entrepreneur Georgio Kosmidis, who has expanded the company to serve up to South-western Ontario.

Accredited, licensed, experienced

ACMO 2000 certified, CMRAO licensed.

Arthex is proud to be an ACMO and CCI corporate member and has obtained the ACMO 2000 certification, which distinguishes professional property management firms. We are pleased to have an A+ Better Business Bureau rating.

Arthex Property Management is registered and licensed by the CMRAO along with all its managers, supported by specialists in accounting, customer service coordination, and professional administration.

Our mission

To provide a harmonious, safe, and peaceful environment ensuring a financially secure future for all the properties that we manage. Quality service is not the main objective in our company, it is the only objective.
