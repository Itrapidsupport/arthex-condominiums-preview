Full-service condo management

Operational support, financial management, administrative services, and maintenance coordination are all handled by our experienced team.

Serving communities across Ontario

We manage condominiums throughout Etobicoke, Mississauga, Oakville, Burlington, Milton, Guelph, Kitchener, Brantford, Hamilton, Stoney Creek, Grimsby, St. Catharines, Niagara-on-the-Lake, Niagara Falls, and Fort Erie.

Administration and financial

18 services
  • Preparing monthly unaudited financial statements
  • Cheque-less payments to all vendors
  • Preparing and issuing work orders and purchase orders
  • Value-added community website, including smartphone application access
  • Yearly budget preparation and action plan
  • Developing a yearly action plan with the reserve fund
  • Condo fee collection and follow-up arrears reports
  • Accounts payable and receivable
  • Arranging and preparing documents for the annual audit
  • Semi-monthly bank deposits and EFTs
  • Preparation of infraction and compliance letters
  • Preparation of notices, newsletters, status certificates and section 98 agreements
  • Preparation of policies for collections, reserve fund, infractions, parking and maintenance
  • 24-hour maximum response to client concerns via phone or email
  • Online member community website for easy access to important condo information
  • Preparing welcome packages for new owners
  • Preparing and issuing PICs, ICUs and NOICs
  • Preparing and issuing preliminary meeting notices, notices of meetings, returns and notices of change

Property management
